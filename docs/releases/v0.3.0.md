# DPIP v0.3.0 — Manasbal Lake

**Release date:** 2026-09-26

## Summary

v0.3.0 turns the post-v0.2 privacy work into a coherent executable evidence boundary. DPIP can now represent material privacy evidence gaps as governed work objects, consume bounded evidence from the Trust Protocol Interop Lab without inflating its evidentiary meaning, and evaluate privacy minimisation at material commitment and predicate-defined audience boundaries while preserving explicit non-inference when runtime evidence is unavailable.

The central release judgment is that privacy evidence is no longer merely a result attached to an examination: the obligation to obtain that evidence, its target, observer, maturity, supplier and claim boundary can itself be validated and handed across repository boundaries.

## Key additions

### Executable privacy evidence obligations

`dpip-evidence-obligation/v1` makes material privacy evidence gaps machine-verifiable. Obligations bind the target revision, observer scope, required maturity, required observations, supplier and blocker state while preserving authority over privacy judgment and residual risk.

Targetless E3+ obligations remain blocked. Operator-only or governance-only material is not misrepresented as runtime evidence. Missing or unavailable observation surfaces remain explicit.

### Human-power privacy evidence consumption

DPIP now consumes the bounded Interop Lab human-power pressure package for privacy-specific propositions while preserving the distinction between observation and broader human-power judgment.

The current synthetic pressure program can support bounded privacy findings for expanded disclosure, stable-identifier demand and cross-session retention, but it does not establish deployment coercion, discrimination, legitimacy or production behavior. Governed counter-cases remain explicit.

### Task-citation and outcome-evidence correlation

DPIP can map task-citation evidence into its privacy observability result contract while distinguishing:

- source/specification characterization;
- fixture-only support;
- target-runtime execution;
- not-implemented surfaces;
- not-observable surfaces.

A stable target-runtime citation join may support a bounded negative privacy disposition. Fixture success, document proof validity or outcome-evidence intent cannot establish privacy PASS without the required runtime observation.

### C9 — minimum-disclosure authority proof at commitment

The new C9 interaction models the privacy consequences of proving authority at the material commitment boundary. It evaluates whether the proof reveals only what is required for the act, whether durable mandate/approval identifiers enlarge correlation scope, and whether approval evidence itself leaks more than necessary.

DPIP does not decide whether authority exists. It evaluates the privacy cost of the composed authority-proof interaction.

### Predicate-defined audience disclosure

A new pressure-test family distinguishes:

- raw group/membership disclosure;
- identifiers whose correlation scope exceeds the required audience;
- audience-scoped minimized membership proof;
- collective-authority cases with no predicate-audience disclosure surface;
- missing observability evidence.

This prevents “membership is required” from becoming an automatic justification for broad membership disclosure.

### Persona reach, usage and timeline examination

The source-pinned persona examination credits implemented reach/minimisation/access controls and records the intentional holder-side cross-context join. External-observer/runtime correlation remains `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` with an exact A/B retest trigger rather than being inferred away from implementation intent.

## Assurance and privacy boundaries

v0.3.0 preserves the following invariants:

- missing required evidence never becomes PASS;
- targetless or blocked runtime obligations remain blocked;
- synthetic evidence does not establish deployment behavior;
- proof validity is not privacy;
- authority success is not minimisation;
- DPIP owns privacy conclusions, not authority, harm, legitimacy, discrimination or whole-portfolio assurance conclusions;
- upstream semantics remain authoritative for the components that define them.

## Upgrade / integration guidance

Existing v0.2 consumers of the portable RAHP specialist result/return contract can continue to interoperate. Integrations that consume the new evidence-obligation or task-citation correlation machinery should preserve the declared provenance class, target binding and observer scope end to end.

Consumers must not reinterpret `BLOCKED`, `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`, `INDETERMINATE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE` or bounded PASS/FAIL results outside the proposition and evidence boundary that produced them.

## Known residuals

This release does not close:

- issuer-side credential-enumeration purpose/retention questions;
- currently targetless human-power runtime obligations;
- external-observer persona correlation without attributable A/B evidence;
- deployment-wide or network/device unlinkability;
- private-room/runtime propositions whose required implementation surfaces do not exist;
- upstream normative gaps DPIP does not own.

These remain future examination or evidence triggers and do not invalidate the bounded capabilities versioned by v0.3.0.
