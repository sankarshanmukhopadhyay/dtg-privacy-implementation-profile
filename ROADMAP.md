# DPIP roadmap

## Current release boundary — v0.2.0 Prashar Lake

v0.2.0 stabilizes attributable runtime evidence handling and explicit privacy-observability boundaries on top of the v0.1 specialist contract. The release makes it easier to distinguish a cryptographically capable component from a composition that has actually demonstrated the named privacy property under attributable evidence.

### Completed in v0.2.0

1. **Attributable A/B runtime evidence** for relationship/verifier, status, Trust Task and policy-discovery privacy propositions.
2. **Bounded SATISFIED outcomes** that retire exact evidence obligations without becoming deployment-wide unlinkability claims.
3. **Data Rooms observability contract** defining E1-E6 evidence expectations and preserving private-tier evidence gaps.
4. **Specialist ownership transfers** so privacy-specific residuals can move from RAHP review threads into DPIP without losing provenance.
5. **Enumeration/privacy boundary** separating successful management authorization from legitimate-purpose, minimization, retention and correlation analysis.
6. **Release/runtime discipline** retaining evidence-required and model-gap outcomes when required observations do not exist.

## Post-v0.2 priorities

1. Acquire real private-room membership/invitation/key-establishment evidence when the required same-subject/common-control and runtime support exists.
2. Complete issuer-side credential-enumeration privacy examination using attributable deployment-role and purpose evidence.
3. Extend network/device observer coverage without conflating protocol-level unlinkability with deployment-level unlinkability.
4. Expand portable evidence collectors and cross-implementation fixtures while preserving immutable source/provenance requirements.
5. Keep specialist-return contracts compatible and versioned independently from any single RAHP release.
6. Add privacy examinations only where DPIP owns the proposition; leave normative component semantics with their upstream owners.

## Non-regression rules

- Missing evidence never becomes PASS.
- Successful authorization is not proof of privacy minimization.
- Cryptographic success is not composition privacy success.
- Synthetic/calibration fixtures do not establish deployment behavior.
- A bounded SATISFIED result does not imply universal unlinkability.
- Upstream semantic uncertainty remains upstream uncertainty rather than being patched by DPIP inference.

## Historical boundary

v0.1.0 **Khajjiar Lake** remains the immutable first governed DPIP release and baseline for the portable specialist contract.
