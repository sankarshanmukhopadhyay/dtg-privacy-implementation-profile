# DPIP roadmap

## Current release boundary — v0.3.0 Manasbal Lake

v0.3.0 stabilizes the post-v0.2 transition from privacy evidence handling to **executable privacy evidence obligations and commitment-bound minimisation analysis**. The release keeps source, fixture, runtime and observer evidence classes distinct while making missing or blocked evidence a first-class machine-verifiable lifecycle condition.

### Completed in v0.3.0

1. **Executable privacy evidence obligations** using `dpip-evidence-obligation/v1`, including target, observer, maturity, supplier, blocker and authority boundaries.
2. **Bounded human-power privacy consumption** that can derive privacy-specific findings from admitted evidence without taking over coercion, legitimacy, discrimination or broader assurance judgment.
3. **Task-citation correlation consumption** that distinguishes source characterization, fixture support, runtime observation and unavailable surfaces and refuses to infer privacy PASS from proof validity alone.
4. **C9 minimum-disclosure authority proofs** at material commitment, including correlation treatment for durable mandate and approval identifiers.
5. **Predicate-defined audience pressure testing** with explicit raw-disclosure, correlation, minimized-proof and missing-evidence cases.
6. **Persona reach/usage/timeline examination** returning current controls and intentional joins as evidence while retaining external observer correlation as an exact future evidence trigger.
7. **CI non-regression coverage** for the new evidence-obligation and task-citation correlation machinery.

## Post-v0.3 priorities

1. Acquire attributable target-runtime evidence for currently blocked E3+ obligations when concrete target deployments become available.
2. Complete issuer-side credential-enumeration privacy examination using attributable deployment-role and legitimate-purpose evidence.
3. Acquire the external-observer A/B evidence required by the persona reach/usage/timeline examination.
4. Extend network/device observer coverage without conflating protocol-level unlinkability with deployment-level unlinkability.
5. Admit additional composed interactions only where DPIP owns the privacy proposition and the evidence contract can be made explicit.
6. Keep evidence-obligation, specialist-return and Interop-consumption contracts versioned independently from any single controller or implementation release.

## Non-regression rules

- Missing evidence never becomes PASS.
- A blocked or targetless runtime obligation is not silently downgraded to fixture evidence.
- Successful authorization is not proof of privacy minimization.
- Cryptographic success is not composition privacy success.
- Synthetic/calibration fixtures do not establish deployment behavior.
- A bounded SATISFIED/PASS result does not imply universal unlinkability.
- Upstream semantic uncertainty remains upstream uncertainty rather than being patched by DPIP inference.
- DPIP privacy judgment does not become authority, harm, legitimacy or deployment-assurance judgment.

## Historical boundaries

v0.2.0 **Prashar Lake** remains the attributable-runtime-evidence and observability baseline.

v0.1.0 **Khajjiar Lake** remains the immutable first governed DPIP release and baseline for the portable specialist contract.
